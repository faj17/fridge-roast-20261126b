One line roasting a smart fridge (92 characters):

> My fridge's camera caught me eating ice cream at 2 a.m. and emailed the photo to my dentist.

The setup is the fridge's in-door camera catching a 2 a.m. ice-cream raid. The turn is that it does not just record the raid — it sends the photo somewhere. The punch is the recipient: the owner's dentist. No milk reordered, nobody locked out — just a snitch appliance with a direct line to the one person who should never see that photo.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
